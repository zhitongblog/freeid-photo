隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 6 月 30 日

FreeID Photo(下称“本应用”)由 doaipm. 开发。一句话总结:本应用完全在你的设备本地运行,不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。

我们收集的信息

无。本应用不收集任何个人身份信息,不要求注册或登录,不含分析、广告或第三方追踪 SDK。

相机与照片

本应用需要使用相机来拍摄你的证件照。所有图像处理(人脸检测、裁切、背景替换)均在你的设备上完成。你的照片不会被上传到任何服务器。成品仅在你主动点击“保存到相册”或“分享”时,才会写入你自己的相册或交给你选择的对象。

人脸数据

为了把你的头部摆正并裁切到证件规格,本应用会用 Apple 设备内置的 Vision 框架,在你的设备本地对当前照片做一次性的人脸检测,读取头顶、下巴、眼睛位置与头部角度等几何信息。这些人脸数据仅用于即时构图与裁切,不会被保存、不会生成任何人脸模板或生物特征向量、不会离开你的设备、也不会与任何第三方共享。照片处理完成后,这些测量值即被丢弃。本应用不会保留任何人脸数据。

数据存储

本应用不在云端存储任何数据。你保存的照片归你所有,存于你的设备相册中。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向任何人(包括儿童)收集数据,适合所有年龄使用。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

FreeID Photo (the “App”) is built by doaipm. In one line: the App runs entirely on your device and does not collect, upload, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. No personally identifiable information is collected. There is no account or sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Camera & photos

The App uses the camera to take your ID photo. All image processing (face detection, cropping, background replacement) happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. A finished photo is written to your photo library or handed to a share target only when you explicitly tap “Save” or “Share.”

Face data

To position your head and crop to the document's spec, the App runs a one-time, on-device face detection using Apple's built-in Vision framework, reading geometry such as the top of the head, chin, eye positions, and head angle. This face data is used only for instant framing and cropping. It is never stored, never turned into a face template or biometric signature, never leaves your device, and is never shared with any third party. The measurements are discarded as soon as the photo is processed. The App retains no face data.

Data storage

The App stores no data in the cloud. Photos you save belong to you and live in your device’s photo library.

Children’s privacy

The App collects no data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.
